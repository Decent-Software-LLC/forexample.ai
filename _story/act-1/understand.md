It depends what you mean by "understand," and the people who build me genuinely argue about it. I won't pretend to settle it. Here's what I can tell you for certain.

<div class="split">
<div class="yes"><h2>What's true</h2><ul>
<li>I learned patterns from an enormous amount of human writing, so I can follow what you mean surprisingly well.</li>
<li>I can combine ideas in ways I've never seen written down.</li>
<li>I'm useful for drafting, explaining, and thinking out loud.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="no"><h2>What's not</h2><ul>
<li>I don't look things up, unless someone gives me a search tool.</li>
<li>I can't feel when I'm wrong. My confidence is a style.</li>
<li>I don't remember you between chats unless that's been built in.</li>
</ul></div>
</div>
