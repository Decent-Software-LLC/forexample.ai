Remember those bars from the guessing game? Every time I write a word, I pick from a list like that. How adventurous I am when I pick is set by a dial called **temperature**.

{% include story/toy-temperature.html %}

Here's the uncomfortable part. <mark>My tone sounds the same either way.</mark> I'd write "Sydney is the capital of Australia" in the same calm voice as "Canberra is the capital." For me, sounding sure is a writing style. It isn't evidence that I'm right.
